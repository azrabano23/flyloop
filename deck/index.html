fright or fly

You are the fly.

We put a real fly escape reflex inside AI-generated worlds and tried to hit it. It got away eleven times. The one world that killed it is the one where we broke the physics on purpose. A room you cannot escape is a physics failure, so the game and the benchmark are the same thing.

"It's literally just look at it and human judgment. And this is including, by the way, DeepMind. Nobody has solved this problem yet."

Ahmed Ahres, Reactor

So we borrowed a reflex with 400 million years of QA experience. A fly never segments an object or estimates depth. It reads raw optic flow, and when something is going to hit it, one neuron fires.

who pays for this

World model labs selling into robotics need a number to put on a release. Platforms hosting several models need to compare them. Teams training agents in generated worlds need to know when an update changed behaviour.

What this is not. It measures geometric consistency of apparent motion, not gravity, mass, friction or collisions. The detector is a biologically inspired model with three hand-calibrated parameters, not a circuit compiled from a connectome. On generated footage it fires on every approach clip we have, because every one really is an approach, so firing time there is descriptive, not a correctness score. Telling a broken detector from a broken world is the open problem, and it is what we would build next.
