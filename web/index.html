flyloop / can you swat the fly?

Fifteen worlds. One hit.

We put a real fly's escape reflex inside AI-generated worlds and sent monsters at it. It dodged fourteen. The only world that killed it was the one where we broke the physics on purpose.

06 / honest limits

What this is not. It measures whether things approach the way geometry says they should. Not gravity, mass, friction or collisions. The circuit is a biologically inspired model with three hand-calibrated parameters, not a connectome. And on generated footage it fires on every real approach, so firing time there describes a world rather than grading it. Telling a broken detector from a broken world is the open problem, and it is what we would build next.
