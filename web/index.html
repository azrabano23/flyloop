flyloop / can you swat the fly?

Twelve worlds. Eleven failed swats.

We put a real fruit fly escape reflex inside AI-generated worlds and tried to hit it. It got away eleven times. The one world that killed it was the one where we had deliberately broken the physics. That is not a game mechanic, that is the result.

03 / the probe

Borrow a reflex with 400 million years of QA.

A fly never segments an object or estimates depth. It reads raw optic flow across a hex array of roughly 750 ommatidia, and when something is going to hit it, one neuron fires and the animal is gone. Small, fast, interpretable, and completely ignorant of what it is looking at. That ignorance is the feature: it cannot be fooled by anything that only looks right.

Two channels, not one

The published anatomy puts two populations on the giant fibre: LC4 keyed to angular velocity, LPLC2 to angular size. We built one channel first and hit a wall, stable threshold or sensitive to broken motion, never both. Two gets both.

No segmentation anywhere

The trigonometry baseline must find an object before it can measure one. Point it at a real generated hallway and it fires in frame zero on carpet shadows.

05 / results

What the second channel buys.

Measured only on clips whose geometry is known in closed form, so there is a right answer to check against. Both detectors are held to the same settling window. The first time we measured this they were not, and the gap that produced was an artefact, so we threw it out.

07 / honest limits

Read this before believing any number above

This measures geometric consistency of apparent motion. It does not measure gravity, mass, friction or collision response. The detector is a biologically inspired model with three hand-calibrated parameters, not a circuit compiled from a connectome; that adapter is a stub. On generated footage the circuit fires on every approach clip we have, because every one of them really is an approach, so firing time there is descriptive rather than a correctness score. Separating a broken detector from a broken world is the open problem, and it is the next thing we would build.
